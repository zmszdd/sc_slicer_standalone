Unprotected `init()` allows builder admin takeover and token drain

## Finding description and impact
The `BuilderWallet.init()` function is an **unprotected external function** with no access control that can be called by anyone, multiple times. This enables an attacker to hijack the `builderAdmin` role and gain complete control of the wallet, including the ability to call `sweep()` and steal all accumulated tokens.


1. `init()` is external with no access control or reentrancy protection
2. Can be called by **anyone** and **multiple times**
3. `builderAdmin` controls who can call `sweep()` and steal tokens

### Attack Flow

**Step 1: Owner initiates deployment**

**Step 2: Owner's call gets mempool visibility**
Attacker sees the pending transaction and can:
1. Calculate the BuilderWallet address using public `predictBuilderWallet(12345)`
2. Front-run or race the owner's `init()` call

**Step 3: Attack execution**

### Impact
- All tokens accumulated in BuilderWallet can be stolen
- Builder fee shares lost
- Protocol loses builder incentive revenue

## Recommended mitigation steps

Move init to constructor.
