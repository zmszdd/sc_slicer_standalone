Bootstrap Shares Cause Permanent Economic Dilution

## Finding Description and Impact
The `HypoVault` contract initializes `totalSupply` with 1,000,000 shares at deployment without allocating the shares to any addresses. This creates "phantom shares" that exist in `totalSupply` but are not owned by anyone.
This problem is compounded by the `fulfillDeposits()` mechanism which first adds new shares to `totalSupply` and then allocates them to users via `executeDeposit()`. This two-step process creates a permanent accounting imbalance.

This violates the fundamental ERC20 standard `totalSupply ≠ sum of all balances`. Phantom shares “steal” a significant proportion of the vault’s value, resulting in users losing 66% of their true value. Share prices are calculated using an inflated `totalSupply` which results in users getting far less than they should.

## Recommended Mitigation Steps
Remove bootstrap shares.
