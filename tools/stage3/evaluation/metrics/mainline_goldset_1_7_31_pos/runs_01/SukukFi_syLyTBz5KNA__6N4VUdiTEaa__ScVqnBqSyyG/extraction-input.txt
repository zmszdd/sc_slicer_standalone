Missing whenNotPaused Modifier in Batch Transfer Functions Bypasses Emergency Stop

## Finding description and impact

The `batchTransfers()` and `rBatchTransfers()` functions (lines 708, 1129) are missing the `whenNotPaused` modifier. While all other transfer functions (`transfer()`, `transferFrom()`, `mint()`, `burn()`) properly enforce pause checks, batch transfers can execute during emergency pauses. This completely defeats the purpose of the emergency stop mechanism, as batch settlements typically represent the largest value operations in the system. The functions' documentation states "Contract must not be paused" but this requirement is not enforced.

**Impact:** During security incidents, regulatory freezes, or disputed settlements, admin cannot halt batch operations. Validator can continue processing settlements even when contract is paused, allowing vulnerable logic to execute, compliance violations to occur, and irreversible settlements during investigations.
