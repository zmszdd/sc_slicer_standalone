The check: rewardTokenMinAmount() is performed on the pre fee campaign amount instead of the post fee amount.

### Finding description and impact

The invariant intended to enforce a minimum reward-per-epoch distribution is incorrectly computed in _createCampaign() inside DistributionCreator. The check uses the before fee removal campaign amount instead of the after fee removal distributed amount, allowing creators to bypass the minimum amount distributed per-epoch requirement.

### Root cause

During campaign creation, the protocol applies fees to the reward amount:


However, the invariant meant to enforce sufficient reward per epoch is evaluated using the original amount, not the distributedAmount:

This produces a logical mismatch:
	•	The check verifies that the pre-fee amount per epoch exceeds the minimum threshold.
	•	But the actual tokens delivered to the Distributor are reduced by fees, resulting in a lower real distribution per epoch.

The PoC demonstrates this flaw:
	1.	A user chooses a reward amount just above the minimum-per-epoch threshold.
	2.	After fee deduction, the real amount becomes lower than the required minimum.
	3.	The campaign still passes validation because the invariant used the higher pre-fee value.

As a result, a campaign can be created with underfunded per-epoch rewards, violating the economic guarantees the invariant was supposed to enforce.

## Impact

This vulnerability enables systematic circumvention of the minimum reward-per-epoch constraints:
	•	Campaign creators can intentionally select an amount that barely meets the invariant, knowing that fees will push the real distribution below the enforced minimum.
	•	Underfunded distributions may lead to:
	•	Inadequate per epoch incentives.
	•	Misleading campaign configurations passed off as compliant.
	•	Potential downstream issues for users expecting a minimum guaranteed payout.


## Recommended mitigation steps

1. Perform invariant checks on the post-fee distributed amount

Replace the current invariant with one using distributedAmount, not amount:
This ensures the check aligns with the actual amount available for reward distribution.
