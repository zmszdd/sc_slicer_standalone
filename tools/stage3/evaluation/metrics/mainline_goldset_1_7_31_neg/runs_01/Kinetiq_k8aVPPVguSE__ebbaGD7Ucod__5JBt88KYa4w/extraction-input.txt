## Finding description and impact

There is a inconsistency in how slashed funds are treated across different parts of the Kinetiq protocol. Specifically, when calculating the staking limit in StakingManager.sol, slashed funds are not considered, but when calculating the exchange ratio in StakingAccountant.sol, slashed funds are subtracted from the total HYPE amount.

## Impact
This inconsistency has several significant impacts:

1. Artificial Staking Limit Restriction: The staking limit check does not account for slashed funds, which means the protocol may prevent new stakes even though the actual amount of HYPE in the system (after accounting for slashing) is below the staking limit. This artificially restricts the protocol's capacity.
2. Inconsistent Protocol Behavior: The protocol treats slashed funds differently in different contexts - they are considered when calculating the exchange ratio but ignored when enforcing the staking limit. This creates confusion and unpredictability in the protocol's behavior.
3. Exchange Ratio Manipulation: The inconsistency could potentially be exploited to manipulate the exchange ratio. Since slashing reduces the total HYPE amount used in the exchange ratio calculation but doesn't affect the staking limit check, an attacker could potentially create scenarios where the exchange ratio is artificially lowered.
4. Capital Inefficiency: During periods of significant slashing, the protocol might artificially limit new stakes, reducing capital efficiency and potentially driving users to other staking solutions.
5. Misleading Protocol Metrics: The inconsistency creates a discrepancy between the reported staked amount used for staking limits and the actual funds in the system used for exchange ratio calculations, which could mislead users and integrators about the protocol's state.
