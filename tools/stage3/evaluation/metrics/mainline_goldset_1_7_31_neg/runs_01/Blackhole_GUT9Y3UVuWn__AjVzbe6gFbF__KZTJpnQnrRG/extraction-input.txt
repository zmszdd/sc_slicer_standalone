Bonus Farming via Repeated SM-NFT Splitting and Merging

## Finding description and impact


The split function in the VotingEscrow contract allows users to divide an existing lock into two separate locks. If the original lock is an SM-NFT, both resulting locks after the split also retain the SM-NFT status. While this may seem reasonable, there is a critical flaw in the bonus handling logic specific to SM-NFTs.

When the SM-NFT is split, the split portion is treated as a fresh SM-NFT and the bonus is applied to it. This results in one of the resulting NFTs contain the inflated token balances due to bonuses. 

An attacker can exploit this by repeatedly merging and splitting SM-NFTs—each time receiving additional bonus tokens that should only have been granted once. By looping this process, the attacker effectively farms unearned bonus tokens and inflates their locked balance far beyond their actual deposit, creating a major economic vulnerability in the system.

## Impact

Unlimited Bonus Farming: The calculate_sm_nft_bonus function is intended to reward users once when they opt into SM-NFT. However, due to its repeated application on every split, attackers can loop the merge-split process to continually receive unearned bonuses. This causes exponential inflation of locked balances from a fixed initial deposit (e.g., 100 tokens can become 200, 300, etc.).

## Severity Classification

**This issue constitutes a high severity vulnerability because it enables any attacker to artificially inflate their locked balance far beyond their actual token deposit through a simple and repeatable process. By repeatedly merging and splitting SM-NFTs, the attacker can continuously farm bonus tokens, the attacker gains an unfair advantage over honest participants.**


## Recommended mitigation steps

Remove the calculate_sm_nft_bonus logic from the split function, even if the lock is marked as an SM-NFT. Users already receive the SM-NFT bonus at the time of lock creation, and reapplying the bonus during a split introduces a critical vulnerability. Allowing the bonus to be added again during each split enables attackers to exploit the system by repeatedly splitting and merging locks to farm unearned bonus tokens.

## Proof of Concept

 Let's understand the exploit with an example:

- The attacker locks X amount of tokens and chooses the SM-NFT option via the create_lock function.(Also note you get bonus by creating a SMNFT lock.).

- Now they split the NFT into two locks by calling the split function. Heres the [link](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-05-blackhole/blob/92fff849d3b266e609e6d63478c4164d9f608e91/contracts/VotingEscrow.sol#L1147) to the split function

- When they split it, the bonus is added again to the new NFT's which were created.


- Now they have two NFTs, both with more value than they should have.

- They merge the two NFTs back into one. (Alternatively, instead of merging, they can keep splitting the already split NFTs repeatedly — each time receiving additional bonus — effectively compounding the exploit.

- Then they split the merged NFT again, getting more bonus added.

- By doing this merge and split over and over, the attacker keeps getting free bonus tokens.

- This trick lets the attacker grow their balance way more than they actually put in, giving them unfair power or rewards.
