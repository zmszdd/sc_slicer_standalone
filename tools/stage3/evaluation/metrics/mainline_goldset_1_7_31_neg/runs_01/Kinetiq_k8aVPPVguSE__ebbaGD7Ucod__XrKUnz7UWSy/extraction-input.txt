Slashing Amount Not Accounted for in Net Staking Limit Check

## Finding description and impact

In the `StakingManager::stake` function the protocol enforces a staking limit by calculating the `netStaked` amount as:


However, it does not subtract the `slashingAmount` of a validator, which leads to an overestimated `netStaked` value. This, in turn, underestimates the remaining staking capacity of a validator. As a result, **new stake attempts may be incorrectly rejected despite the validator having sufficient actual capacity**.

So let's say a validator has a staking limit of `100 HYPE`.
- A: stakes `50 HYPE`
- B: stakes `20 HYPE`
- totalStaked = `70 HYPE`

If the validator is slashed `20 HYPE`, the effective stake is now only `50 HYPE`. However, since slashing isn’t deducted in `netStaked`, the protocol still considers it as `70 HYPE`, leaving only `30 HYPE` of capacity, when it should be `50 HYPE`.

This miscalculation can lead to denial of service for users trying to stake and results in inefficient utilization of the protocol’s staking limits.

## Recommended mitigation steps

Make sure to add the `slashingAmount` in the `netStake` calculation
