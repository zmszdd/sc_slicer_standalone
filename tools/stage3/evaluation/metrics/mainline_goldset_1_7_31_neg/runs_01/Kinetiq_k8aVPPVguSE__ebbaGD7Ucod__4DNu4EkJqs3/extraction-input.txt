Missing slashing consideration in staking limit leads to incorrect capacity tracking

## Vulnerability Details
In [StakingManager::stake()](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-04-kinetiq/blob/7f29c917c09341672e73be2f7917edf920ea2adb/src/StakingManager.sol#L230-L237), the staking limit calculation includes rewards but ignores slashing events when determining available capacity:


The calculation only subtracts `totalClaimed` from `totalStaked + rewardsAmount` but does not consider slashing events. When validators are slashed, the actual staked amount decreases, which should increase available capacity. By not accounting for slashing, the staking limit remains artificially lower than it should be.

## Impact
Users are prevented from staking even when actual staked amount is below limit due to slashing.


## Recommendation
Include slashing in net staked calculation

## Proof of Concept

1. Initial state:
- `stakingLimit = 1000 HYPE` 
- `totalStaked = 800 HYPE`
- `rewardsAmount = 100 HYPE`
- `totalClaimed = 0 HYPE`

2. Validator is slashed for 200 HYPE:
- Actual staked amount = 600 HYPE
- `netStaked = 800 + 100 - 0 = 900 HYPE` (slashing not considered)

3. User tries to stake 150 HYPE:

4. Even though actual staked amount is only 600 HYPE, the stake is rejected due to not considering slashing.
