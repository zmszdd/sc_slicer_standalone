Permanent DoS in unregisterVault() blocked by 1-wei dust

## Finding description
```ShareTokenUpgradeable.unregisterVault()``` assumes a vault’s ERC-20 token balance can be made exactly zero and enforces
Because ERC-20 transfers are permissionless, any external actor can send dust (1 wei) to the vault at any time. In async/pooled vault designs (ERC-7575, rounding, cancellation flows, partial settlements), tiny residuals are common. The strict balance check therefore allows a trivial, permissionless griefing attack


## impact
- Permanent denial-of-service of unregisterVault() for the target vault
- Permanent consumption of one vault slot protocol may be unable to register new assets (global DoS of multi-asset onboarding).

## Recommended mitigation steps

Replace raw balance check with accounting-based validation
