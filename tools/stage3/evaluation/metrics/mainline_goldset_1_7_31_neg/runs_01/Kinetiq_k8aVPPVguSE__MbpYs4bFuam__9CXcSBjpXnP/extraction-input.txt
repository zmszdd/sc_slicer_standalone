External Call Failure Handling Allows Execution with Insufficient Oracle Responses

## Finding description and impact 
In the `OracleManager.generatePerformance` function, a try-catch block is utilized to handle failures when calling `IOracleAdapter.getPerformance`, which causes any failed oracle response to be skipped. The relevant snippet of code is:


This mechanism does not differentiate between scenarios where only a minimal number of oracle responses (potentially as low as one, based on `MIN_VALID_ORACLES`) are successful and those where a majority of oracle calls fail. As a result, even with multiple active oracles, the execution may proceed with partial or potentially anomalous data, thereby risking inaccurate performance calculations that may lead to inappropriate rewards or slashing events affecting validator funds.

#### Impact  
Partial or inconsistent data aggregation could lead to inaccurate validator performance updates, potentially causing fund loss due to mismanaged rewards or slashing events.

## Recommended mitigation steps
Enforce a higher minimum threshold for valid oracle responses or require that a specific percentage of active oracles succeed before proceeding.
