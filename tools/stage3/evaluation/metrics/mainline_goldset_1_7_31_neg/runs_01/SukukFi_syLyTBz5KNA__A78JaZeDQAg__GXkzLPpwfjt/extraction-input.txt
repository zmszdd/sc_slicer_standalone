`getCirculatingSupplyAndAssets` Relies on Unstable `rBalance`, Leading to Incorrect Share Conversions

## Summary

The function `getCirculatingSupplyAndAssets` is used during share-to-asset and asset-to-share conversions. It relies on `totalInvestmentAssets`, which includes `rBalance` from the investment share token. However, `rBalance` is not stable and can be reversed via `WERC7575ShareToken::cancelrBalanceAdjustment`. If `rBalance` is later corrected, users whose deposits or redemptions were executed based on the incorrect value will receive inaccurate share amounts or asset amounts, effectively causing value shifts between users.

## Root Cause

`_calculateInvestmentAssets` adds both the investment share token balance and its `rBalance`:


However, `rBalance` is not immutable. It can be reverted through `cancelrBalanceAdjustment`:


Because deposits and redemptions use this value at execution time, any later correction to `rBalance` introduces inconsistency and unintended wealth transfer between users.

## Mitigation

Do not include `rBalance` in investment asset calculations, as these balances are not realized. Only add them once they are fully realized and reflected in the actual vault asset balance. This prevents reversible or incorrect `rBalance` adjustments from corrupting share conversion logic.
