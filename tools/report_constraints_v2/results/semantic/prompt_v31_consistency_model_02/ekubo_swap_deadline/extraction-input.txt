## Impact
Transaction expiration is a standard security practice in AMMs (like Uniswap) to prevent miners or validators from withholding a transaction and executing it at a later time when the market conditions are unfavorable to the user.

The `Router` swap functions lack a `deadline` or `timestamp` check. 

If a user submits a swap and the gas price spikes (or a validator holds the tx), the transaction can remain pending in the mempool for an indefinite period. When it is finally executed (e.g., hours or days later), the price might have moved significantly, leading to unexpected losses for the user that could have been prevented with a simple expiration check.
