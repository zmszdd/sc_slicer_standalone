Flawed Rewards Termination Logic Allows Early Buyers to Extract AMM Trading Fees Indefinitely

## Finding description and impact

The GTE launchpad system includes a rewards mechanism where early buyers during the bonding curve phase earn a share of future trading fees. The LaunchToken contract tracks these early buyers through `bondingShare` mappings and `totalFeeShare` counters. When users transfer their tokens away, the system should end the rewards program once no bonding shares remain.

However, the `LaunchToken` contract contains a flawed boolean condition in the `_decreaseFeeShares()` function that prevents the rewards system from properly ending after token graduation. This allows early bonding curve buyers to continue earning AMM trading fees indefinitely, even though rewards should stop when the token graduates to automated market maker trading.

The problem occurs because of the token lifecycle in the launchpad system. During the bonding curve phase, `unlocked` is false. However, when the token graduates to AMM trading, the `unlock()` function is called:


Once `unlocked` becomes true, the condition `if (totalFeeShare == 0 && !unlocked)` can never be satisfied again. The expression `!unlocked` evaluates to false when `unlocked` is true, making the entire condition `(totalFeeShare == 0 && false)` always evaluate to false, regardless of the `totalFeeShare` value.

The `_endRewards()` function exists and is designed to stop the fee collection mechanism:


This function calls the launchpad's `endRewards()`, which ultimately calls `distributor.endRewards(pair)` and then `pair.endRewardsAccrual()` to stop fee collection from AMM swaps. However, due to the flawed condition, this cleanup never happens after graduation.

This makes early bonding curve buyers continue receiving AMM trading fees indefinitely after token graduation.

The rewards system was meant to compensate early risk-takers during the uncertain bonding phase, not provide permanent income streams from mature tokens with established liquidity.

## Recommended mitigation steps

Fix the boolean logic in the `_decreaseFeeShares()` function by removing the `!unlocked` condition, allowing rewards to end when all bonding shares are exhausted regardless of the token's locked status


This change ensures that when all early buyers have transferred away their tokens, the rewards program properly terminates and stops extracting fees from AMM traders.
