createCampaign fails to enforce that duration is a multiple of EPOCH_DURATION, leading to potential reward calculation errors

## Finding description and impact

The `CampaignParameter` struct documentation in `CampaignParameters.sol` explicitly states strict requirements for the duration parameter:



However, the `DistributionCreator.sol` contract (specifically `_createCampaign` and `overrideCampaign`) only enforces the minimum duration, but fails to enforce the "multiple of EPOCH_DURATION" requirement.



https://docs.merkl.xyz/merkl-mechanisms/features?q=computation+schedule#campaigns-of-any-duration

### Impact 

- Data Consistency & Reward Calculation Errors: The Merkl protocol relies on discrete 1-hour epochs (EPOCH_DURATION) for reward calculation and distribution.

 - Partial Epochs: If a creator sets a duration of 5400 seconds (1.5 hours), the campaign covers one full epoch and one half-epoch.

- Off-chain Failure: If the off-chain indexing engine strictly expects aligned epochs, it may either reject the campaign entirely (locking user funds) or drop the final partial epoch (causing a loss of user rewards)
