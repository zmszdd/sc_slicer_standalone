DOMAIN_SEPARATOR will be invalid after chain forks

## Finding description and impact

The `TrailsIntentEntrypoint` contract computes its EIP-712 `DOMAIN_SEPARATOR` once in the constructor and stores it as an immutable value. Because `block.chainid` is read once at deployment, the stored `DOMAIN_SEPARATOR` permanently embeds the original chain ID. If the chain later forks or the chain ID changes, this cached domain separator becomes outdated. As a result, the contract will continue validating signatures against an incorrect chain ID.

This causes all off-chain signatures to become invalid on the new chain, breaking signature-based flows such as `depositToIntent` and `depositToIntentWithPermit`.

Any signature generated on the new chain will not match the original domain separator. This creates a permanent denial-of-service for intent verification on the forked chain.
