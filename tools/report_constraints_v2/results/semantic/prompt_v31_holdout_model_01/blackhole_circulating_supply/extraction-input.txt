## Finding description and impact
In the `update_period` function the `calculate_rebase` function is used that firstly calculates the circulating supply, then based on the calculated amount calculates the rebase amount.
The following snippet is from `calculate_rebase`:
We can see that supply in voting escrow is calculated on amount of tokens inside `VotingEscrow` contract + smNFTBalance + bonus based on that smNFTBalance. The total supply of black is calculated on totalSupply of black + ONLY bonus from smNFT. This is incorrect as smNFTBalance should be taken into consideration when calculating blackSupply. This leads to circulatingBlack variable being equal to `blackTotal - (veTotal + smNFTBalance)`. We can clearly see that this calculation is incorrect, leading to integer underflow whenever 50% of all black is locked inside VotingEscrow. Integer underflow is the highest impact here as it leads to DOS, but also this incorrect calculation leads to incorrect estimation of rebaseAmount inside the MinterUpgradeable.
