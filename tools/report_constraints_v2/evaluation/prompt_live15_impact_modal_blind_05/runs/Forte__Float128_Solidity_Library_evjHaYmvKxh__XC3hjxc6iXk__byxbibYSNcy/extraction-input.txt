The calculation of `sqrt()` is incorrect

## Finding description and impact

The `sqrt()` function returns incorrect result in some case. When number is large, then we calculate its sqrt using `Uint512.mul256x256` and `Uint.sqrt512`.

The issue arises while calculating the exponent of result. In following code, when `rMan` is larger than `MAX_L_DIGIT_NUMBER`, we divide `rMan` by 10 and add 1 to `rExp`. Then `rExp` is divided by 2 to find the exponent of the result, since we calculate the sqrt. As you can see, incresement of 1 doesn't effect anything to division of `rExp`. As result, the rExp wont' be increased by 1 in this case.


**Impact:** High, Compared with the loss of funds in other protocol, the difference in exponent is very high impact in calculation.
