Broken `prevOrderId` Link Orphans Orders and Permanently Locks Maker Collateral

## Summary

Every time a limit order is appended to an existing price-level, the contract fails to persist the new order’s `prevOrderId` field. Subsequent cancellation, full fill, or expiry of that appended order causes the removal routine to mis-handle the doubly-linked list, detaching all earlier orders at that price. 

These ghost orders are no longer reachable by matching, amending, or cancelling logic, yet they continue to:
(a) hold user collateral hostage
(b) inflate open-interest accounting, and
(c) consume one of the limited tree nodes governed by `maxNumLimitsPerSide`

A motivated trader can intentionally create thousands of ghost orders, exhausting book capacity and freezing deep-book activity.
