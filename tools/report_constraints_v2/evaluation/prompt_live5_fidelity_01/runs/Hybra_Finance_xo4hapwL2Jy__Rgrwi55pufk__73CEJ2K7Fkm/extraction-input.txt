multiSplit Causes Permanent Accounting Drift for Permanent Locks

## Summary
Splitting a permanent veNFT via `multiSplit()` allocates child amounts with floor division and does not reconcile `permanentLockBalance`, causing (1) deterministic rounding loss of locked value and (2) global permanent accounting drift.

## Finding Description
`multiSplit()` burns the parent veNFT and mints multiple child veNFTs with proportional amounts calculated by integer division. For a parent with amount `A` and weights `w[i]`, each child receives `floor(A * w[i] / Σw)`. This truncation yields Σ(child) ≤ A and usually Σ(child) < A when `A` is not exactly divisible by `Σw`. The parent is first burned and then children are minted, so the rounding remainder is lost on-chain.

When the parent is a permanent lock (`originalLocked.isPermanent == true`), `multiSplit()` never adjusts the global `permanentLockBalance` to reflect the (smaller) sum of the new child amounts. As a result, `permanentLockBalance` remains at its pre-split value, while the true sum of permanent child locks is smaller due to rounding. This breaks the invariant that `permanentLockBalance` equals the sum of all permanently locked amounts and contaminates global voting-supply snapshots (via `_checkpoint`, which records `last_point.permanent = permanentLockBalance`).

Security guarantees broken:
- Conservation of locked value in splits: Σ(child amounts) must equal the parent’s amount.
- Accuracy of global permanent accounting: `permanentLockBalance` must track the real sum of permanent locks to ensure correct supply snapshots and downstream weighting.

Relevant code:
- Burn parent, checkpoint, and mint children with floor division:
  - ve33/contracts/VotingEscrow.sol:1162–1189
- Global permanent included in checkpoints:
  - ve33/contracts/VotingEscrow.sol:733
- No permanent reconciliation in `multiSplit`.

## Impact Explanation
Medium impact:
- On-chain locked value is lost deterministically (rounding “dust”) upon splitting; users cannot recover it.
- `permanentLockBalance` becomes larger than the true permanent total, skewing global accounting and analyses that rely on accurate permanent weights (e.g., supply curves, voting snapshots).
- The drift accumulates each time permanent locks are split, affecting protocol-wide accounting.

## Likelihood Explanation
High likelihood:
- The behavior is triggered by normal user actions (splitting a permanent veNFT) without special timing or adversarial setup.
- Any split into ≥2 children under non-divisible allocations produces rounding loss.
- No safeguards currently adjust `permanentLockBalance` in `multiSplit`.
