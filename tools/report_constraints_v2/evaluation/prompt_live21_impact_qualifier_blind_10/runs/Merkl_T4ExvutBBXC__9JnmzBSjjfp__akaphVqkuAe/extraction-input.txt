Fee-on-Transfer tokens cause insolvency in campaign funding, DoS on final claim

- Impact
- Final claim permanently reverts on campaigns funded with Fee-on-Transfer ERC20s; recipients cannot withdraw the remaining rewards.
- Distributor’s balance ends up strictly less than the recorded campaign amount; funds remain stuck until governance intervenes, undermining distribution integrity.
- Root Cause
- In createCampaign the system computes campaignAmountMinusFees and then sets newCampaign.amount = campaignAmountMinusFees without validating what the distributor actually received. For FoT tokens, safeTransferFrom / safeTransfer delivers requested - fee_on_transfer , so distributor receives less than newCampaign.amount .
- Distributor.claim computes toSend = amount - claimed[user][token].amount from the Merkle amounts and attempts to safeTransfer(toSend) from its balance. When cumulative claims equal the recorded campaign amount but the distributor holds less (due to FoT deductions), the final claim reverts for insufficient balance.
- Attack Scenario
- Creator approves and calls createCampaign with a FoT reward token and amount A.
- DistributionCreator records newCampaign.amount = A - protocol_fee and transfers that nominal net to the distributor via safeTransferFrom .
- Because the token is FoT, distributor actually receives (A - protocol_fee) - transfer_fee .
- Claims proceed; early ones succeed. The last claim attempts to drain the remainder to reach newCampaign.amount and reverts because the distributor balance is lower than the recorded amount.
- Recommended Mitigation Steps
- Measure the distributor’s actual balance delta and record that value:
  - before = IERC20(rewardToken).balanceOf(distributor)
  - perform fee and distributor transfers
  - after = IERC20(rewardToken).balanceOf(distributor)
  - set newCampaign.amount = after - before
- Apply the same delta-based approach for fee transfers, or explicitly document fee behavior when FoT tokens are used.
- Alternatively:
  - Denylist FoT tokens as reward tokens,
  - Require pre-deposit and validate delta before recording,
  - Document support only for non-FoT ERC20s.
