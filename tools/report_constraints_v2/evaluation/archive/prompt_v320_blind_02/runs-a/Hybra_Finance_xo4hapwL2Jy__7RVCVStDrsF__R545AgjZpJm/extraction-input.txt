Incorrect Share Calculation Leads to Loss of Funds for Subsequent Depositors

## Finding description and impact

The root cause of the vulnerability is a flawed order of operations within the `GrowthHYBR` contract's `deposit()` function. The function first increases the vault's total assets by locking the new user's deposit into the `veNFT`, and only afterward does it calculate the number of shares the user should receive. This causes the depositor's own funds to be included in the `totalAssets` denominator of the share price calculation, severely and unfairly reducing the number of shares they are minted.

**The execution flow is as follows:**

A user deposits `amount`. The contract first calls `IVotingEscrow.deposit_for()`, which adds `amount` to the locked assets. It then calls `calculateShares(amount)`, which uses the now-inflated `totalAssets` value. This results in the formula `shares = amount * old_totalSupply / (old_totalAssets + amount)`, penalizing the depositor.


The impacts of this vulnerability are critical:

1.  Any user depositing after the first will receive significantly fewer shares than they are entitled to, resulting in an immediate and permanent loss of principal.
2.  A malicious first depositor can deposit a minimal amount (e.g., 1 wei) to manipulate the share price. This causes the next depositor, regardless of their deposit size (e.g., 1000 HYBR), to receive zero shares due to integer division, leading to a total loss of their funds.
3.  The contract's core economic function is broken. Instead of a fair yield-bearing vault, it acts as a predatory mechanism that benefits the first depositor at the expense of all subsequent users.
4.  This flaw renders the `GrowthHYBR` contract unusable and poses a critical risk of asset loss to its users.
