Users Can Bypass Performance Fees Through Share Transfers

## Summary

Users can bypass performance fees by transferring profitable shares instead of withdrawing, causing the protocol to lose 100% of its performance fee revenue on profitable positions.

## Root cause

The `transfer()` function only performs regular ERC20 transfers without calculating and deducting performance fees as the withdrawal function does.

`function transfer` is incomplete - allows profit inheritance without fee assessment.

## Attack path 

1. User deposits when vault NAV = X
2. Vault generates profit, NAV = Y (where Y > X)
3. User's shares now worth more than their basis
4. User transfers shares to accomplice instead of withdrawing
5. Performance fee completely bypassed
6. Accomplice can withdraw profit without fee

## Impact

The protocol loses its entire revenue stream from performance fees because users can bypass the fee system through simple share transfers, making the vault business model unsustainable.
>No fees = No revenue = Broken business model
