Builder Fees Can Be Permanently Locked Due to Unsafe ERC20 transfer use in sweep Function.

## Description:  
The `sweep` function in the `BuilderWallet` contract relies on `IERC20.transfer` returning a **boolean** value. This assumption is unsafe for non-standard ERC20 tokens such as USDT, which do not return a **boolean** on `transfer`. When builder fees are distributed in such tokens, the `sweep` call will revert despite the `transfer` succeeding at the token level, causing the withdrawal to fail.

## Impact:  
If the underlying asset is a non-standard ERC20, protocol-generated builder fees transferred to the `BuilderWallet` become permanently inaccessible, preventing the builder from withdrawing earned rewards and resulting in fund stuck for the builder who wants to earn fee

**Severity:**  Medium, as protocol-generated builder fees can become permanently inaccessible, preventing the builder from withdrawing earned rewards
