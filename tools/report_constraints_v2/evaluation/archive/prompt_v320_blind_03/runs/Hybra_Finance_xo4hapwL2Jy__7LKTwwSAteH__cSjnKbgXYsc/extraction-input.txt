Emissions minted with zero total weight become permanently stuck

## Finding description and impact

**Description:** `MinterUpgradeable.update_period()` always mints and transfers `_gauge` HYBR to `GaugeManager.notifyRewardAmount`. When `IVoter.totalWeight()` is zero, `_ratio` stays zero, so `index` never increases and `claimable[gauge]` remains unchanged. The minted tokens accumulate in the gauge manager with no mechanism to distribute or claw them back.

**Impact:** Any actor can call `update_period()` during an epoch where nobody has voted (e.g., launch week or after a mass `reset`). A full epoch’s emissions are stranded inside `GaugeManager`, starving all gauges and breaking the incentives while all transactions appear successful.
