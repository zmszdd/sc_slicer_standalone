FRouter Oracle (getAmountsOut) Provides Persistently Incorrect Prices Due to Stale kLast in FPair

## Finding description and impact

FRouter.getAmountsOut computes price strictly from current reserves and constant-product K without any stabilization or TWAP smoothing.

The FPair.kLast() function erroneously returns the initial constant product value because FPair.swap() does not update _pool.k with the product of the new reserves. This causes FRouter.getAmountsOut() to use a stale k alongside current reserves, leading to fundamentally incorrect price calculations after any trade. While reserve-altering operations are restricted to the EXECUTOR_ROLE, this design flaw makes the getAmountsOut view function an unreliable oracle for any observer or downstream contract, presenting a risk beyond simple privilege misuse as the reported price is persistently inaccurate.

The direct "attack" of skewing reserves is performed by a privileged EXECUTOR_ROLE. Assuming this role is fully trusted and its actions are always considered beneficial or by design, one might argue for a lower severity (like QA for centralization risk: "Direct misuse of privileges shall be submitted in the QA report"). However, the oracle getAmountsOut is a public view function. Anyone can read this skewed price after an executor's trade. The fundamental brokenness of kLast means the oracle is unreliable regardless of intent.
