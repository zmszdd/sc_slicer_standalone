## Finding description and impact
`AtomWallet` is an ERC-4337 compliant Account Abstraction wallet. The owner of the `AtomWallet` can sign  UserOperation and send it to a Bundler. The Bundler will verify the UserOperation and execute it on sender's behalf through `EntryPoint`.
When verifying the signature of `UserOperation`, `EntryPoint` will check returned `validAfter` and `validUntil`. The `UserOperation` can not be executed if the valid period is expired. As can be seen, the validation of `UserOperation` fails once the valid period is expired.

However, Both `validUntil` and `validAfter` can be manipulated, the AtomWallet fails to enforce validUntil and validAfter constraints, permitting the execution of UserOperations outside of their intended validity period.

The root cause is that `validUntil` and `validAfter` are excluded from the UserOperation signature hash. Consequently, these values can be modified without invalidating the signature.
