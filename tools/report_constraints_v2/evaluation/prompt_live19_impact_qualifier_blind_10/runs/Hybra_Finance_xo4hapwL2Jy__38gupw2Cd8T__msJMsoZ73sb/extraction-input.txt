Withdrawers Receive Less Than Fair Share of Assets Due to Incomplete Valuation

## Finding Description  
The GovernanceHYBR (gHYBR) contract acts as a vault where users deposit HYBR to receive gHYBR shares, with assets locked in a veNFT and rewards compounded periodically. However, the [`totalAssets()` ](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-10-hybra-finance/blob/74c2b93e8a4796c1d41e1fbaa07e40b426944c44/ve33/contracts/GovernanceHYBR.sol#L257-L264)function undervalues the vault by only accounting for HYBR locked in the veNFT, excluding pending HYBR balances in the contract (from uncompounded rebases, penalties, or swapped rewards) and any unswapped reward tokens. This leads to incorrect conversions in calculateAssets(shares), used during withdrawals to determine the proportional HYBR amount split from the veNFT. In the withdraw function, after burning shares, it computes hybrAmount = calculateAssets(shares), then splits the veNFT based solely on this undervalued amount, fees, and remaining locked balance—leaving pending assets undistributed and benefiting remaining holders. The root cause is that totalAssets solely queries the veNFT's locked amount via IVotingEscrow(votingEscrow).locked(veTokenId).amount, without incorporating contract-held HYBR or other assets. The highest impact scenario occurs when significant pending HYBR (P > 0) accumulates before a withdrawal, causing the withdrawer to lose their proportional claim on P, with losses scaling with P's size relative to locked assets and potentially reaching substantial portions if compounding is delayed.


In the totalAssets function of GrowthHYBR.sol, the return value is limited to the veNFT's locked HYBR amount, excluding any pending HYBR in the contract or unswapped rewards, breaking the vault's asset valuation invariant.

1. Rewards accumulate in the contract via claimRewards, increasing rebase or votingYield without immediate compounding (e.g., operator delays compound).  
2. A user calls withdraw(shares) to exit their position.  
3. The function computes `hybrAmount` using `calculateAssets(shares), which relies on undervalued totalAssets excluding pending balances.  
4. The veNFT is split based on this undervalued amount, transferring only the locked proportion to the user while leaving pending assets in the contract for remaining holders.

## Impact:  
Withdrawers permanently lose their fair proportional share of pending HYBR and rewards, with higher impact when large uncompounded balances exist (e.g., during high reward periods or operator inaction), though lesser dilution occurs universally on every withdrawal if any pendings are present.
