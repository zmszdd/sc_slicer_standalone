Impact price calculation includes expired orders, which can cause the mark price to be inaccurate in low-liquidity conditions

## Finding description and impact

`_cacheImpactPrice` runs every time the admin sets mark price. The impact price average above depends on `quoteBidInQuote`/`quoteAskInQuote`, which traverse each limit via `_getBaseLimit`. During traversal, no call to `Order.isExpired()` or `_removeUnfillableOrder` happens, so expired orders remain in the queue and still contribute to `baseAmount`. Thus the impact price snapshot includes liquidity that would never execute.




Because mark price is the median of funding component, index+basis EMA, and impact price (`Market.sol:248`), this manipulation flows straight into `markPrice`. Funding payments, liquidation checks (`ClearingHouse.assertNotLiquidatable`), minimum margin requirements, and other safety checks that rely on `markPrice` can become false.
