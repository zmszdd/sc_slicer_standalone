Late-epoch bonding allows users to capture disproportionate epoch rewards

## Finding description and impact

----

### Summary

Rewards for each epoch are calculated using the user's veTRUST balance at the **end of the epoch**. Because the system relies on a single timestamp snapshot, users who bond immediately before epoch close can receive the same reward share as users who participated throughout the entire epoch.

This allows attackers to capture a disproportionate share of emissions while only participating for a negligible portion of the epoch.

----

### Root Cause

Reward calculation uses the user balance at epoch end:

`_userEligibleRewardsForEpoch()`
[File: src/protocol/emissions/TrustBonding.sol#L475-L492](https://github.com/code-423n4/2026-03-intuition/blob/314b7d4d9ccbaf27e4484a6c0706af83d3f75f36/src/protocol/emissions/TrustBonding.sol#L475-L492)
The balance is obtained from:

`userBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd()`
[File: src/protocol/emissions/TrustBonding.sol#L203-L213](https://github.com/code-423n4/2026-03-intuition/blob/314b7d4d9ccbaf27e4484a6c0706af83d3f75f36/src/protocol/emissions/TrustBonding.sol#L203-L213)
Because balances are evaluated only at the epoch end timestamp, the system ignores **how long the user held veTRUST during the epoch**.

----

### Attack Path

1. Honest users bond at the beginning of the epoch.
2. Attacker waits until the last seconds of the epoch.
3. Attacker bonds a large amount of TRUST with a long lock.
4. Epoch ends and rewards are calculated using the attacker's large veTRUST balance.
5. Attacker receives a majority of emissions despite participating briefly.

This strategy can be repeated every epoch.

----

### Impact

Late bonding allows attackers to dilute rewards for honest participants.

Example:

Alice bonds `1,000 TRUST` at epoch start.  
Bob bonds `9,000 TRUST` **1 second before epoch end**.

At epoch close:

`Alice ≈ 1,000 veTRUST`  
`Bob ≈ 9,000 veTRUST`

Reward distribution:

`Bob ≈ 90% of emissions`  
`Alice ≈ 10%`

Despite Alice participating for the entire epoch, Bob captures the majority of rewards after bonding only seconds before epoch close.
