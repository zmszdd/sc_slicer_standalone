The function rebaseOraclePack always resets the lockMode to ‘0’

## Finding description and impact
In the OraclePackLibrary, UPPER_118BITS_MASK is wrongly defined as Upper 130 bits mask as it contains 130 ones and 126 zeroes. It is used in the ‘function rebaseOraclePack’ where it just affects the lockMode, which is reset to ‘0’, the corresponding 2 bits lockMode is always ‘00’ after the function rebaseOraclePack gets executed.


The portion “(uint24(_newReferenceTick) & BITMASK_UINT22) << 96)” corrupts the lockMode as the 22 bits referenceTick is typecasted to 24 bits whose upper 2 bits are always zero. 
It’s luck that the lower 10 bits of eonsEMA are not getting modified, but were candidate for getting disturbed due to the wrong mask of 130 bits instead of required 118 bits. In short, rebaseOraclePack unintentionally clears lockMode, but does not corrupt EMA fields.

In the process, the ‘function rebaseOraclePack’ covertly and silently does the work of ‘function unlockPool’ which onlyGuardian is allowed to do, thereby exposing the pool for continual attack which the ‘onlyGuardian’ tried to prevent by setting the lockMode as ‘3’.
