Insufficient Liquidity for Later Withdrawals Due to Withdrawal Amount Calculation.

## Finding description and impact

The StakingManager contract has a critical issue with how withdrawal amounts are calculated and processed, which can lead to insufficient liquidity for later withdrawals after a slashing event. The root cause is that withdrawal amounts are calculated at the time of queueing rather than at confirmation time, which creates an unfair distribution of remaining funds after slashing.

### Attack Path

Ignoring the rewards for the simplier calculations

- User1 and User2 both stake 100 HYPE tokens each, receiving 100 kHYPE tokens each.
- Total HYPE in the system: 200 tokens
- Total kHYPE supply: 200 tokens
- Exchange rate: 1 kHYPE = 1 HYPE

Now User1 queues a withdrawal of 100 kHYPE tokens:
- Calculated HYPE to return: 100 HYPE tokens
- This amount is stored in User1's withdrawal request

A slashing event occurs, penalizing 60 HYPE tokens:
- New total HYPE in the system: 140 tokens
- Total kHYPE supply: 200 tokens (unchanged because user1's tokens are not burned yet) 
- New exchange rate: 1 kHYPE = 0.7 HYPE

User2 now queues a withdrawal of 100 kHYPE tokens:
- Calculated HYPE to return: 70 HYPE tokens
- This amount is stored in User2's withdrawal request

After the withdrawal delay, User1 confirms and receives 100 HYPE tokens:
- Remaining HYPE in the system: 40 tokens

When User2 tries to confirm their withdrawal for 70 HYPE tokens:
- Only 40 HYPE tokens remain in the contract
- The transaction will revert due to insufficient balance

### Impact

1. Users who attempt to withdraw after slashing events may be unable to receive their funds.
2. Unfair Distribution of Slashing Penalties
