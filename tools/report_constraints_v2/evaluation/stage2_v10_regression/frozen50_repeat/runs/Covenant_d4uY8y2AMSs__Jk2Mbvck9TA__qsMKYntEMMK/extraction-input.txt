First over-threshold minter can halt new deposits for all users

## Summary
Seeding `lastETWAPBaseSupply` to zero during market init will cause a denial of service for depositors as the first minter to cross the `noCapLimit` threshold will permanently trip the mint cap until the ETWAP decays.

## Root Cause
In `src/lex/latentswap/LatentSwapLEX.sol:268` the exchange state seeds `lastETWAPBaseSupply` to `0`, and the first mint does not update the stored exponential TWAP before `_checkMintCap` enforces limits. Combined with `_checkMintCap` in `src/lex/latentswap/libraries/LatentSwapLogic.sol:1054`, the system compares the live supply against a near-zero TWAP, so any follow-up mint after the threshold must fail until the ETWAP catches up.


1. Market init seeds ETWAP to zero:
   *Stored ETWAP starts at `0` even though the next mint will add liquidity.*

2. First large mint updates live supply but ETWAP stays ≈0:
   *During `_calculateMarketState`, the TWAP update runs before the mint adds to `baseTokenSupply`, so the exponential moving average remains near zero.*

3. Subsequent mint is capped against the stale ETWAP:
   *`marketState.baseTokenSupply` now exceeds `1 << noCapLimit`, but `lastETWAPBaseSupply` is still ≈0, making the condition `marketBaseTokenSupply + mintAmount <= lastETWAPBaseSupply << 1` impossible and reverting with `E_LEX_MintCapExceeded`.*

4. ETWAP only recovers with a 30-minute half-life:
   *Even a manual `updateState` call takes ~30 minutes to grow ETWAP by 50%, leaving the market effectively frozen in the interim.*


## Impact
New deposits are blocked for roughly an hour each time someone adds just over one token, preventing organic liquidity growth.
