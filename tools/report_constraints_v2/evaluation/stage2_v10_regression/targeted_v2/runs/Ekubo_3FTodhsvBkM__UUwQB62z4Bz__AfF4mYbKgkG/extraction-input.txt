Router lacks transaction deadline protection

## Finding description and impact
The `Router` contract (and the underlying `Core` contract) does not allow users to specify a deadline for their swap transactions. In decentralized exchanges, it is standard practice to include a deadline parameter to prevent transactions from being executed after a significant delay. Without a deadline, a transaction can be held by a validator or remain in the mempool for an extended period and be executed when market conditions have changed unfavorably for the user.

While the `Router` provides slippage protection via `calculatedAmountThreshold` and `sqrtRatioLimit`, these only protect against price movements relative to the execution time. They do not protect against the "time value" of the trade or external factors that might make the trade undesirable after a certain time (e.g., an arbitrage opportunity that is no longer valid, or a user changing their mind).

**Impact:**
Users may have their transactions executed at unexpected times, potentially leading to unwanted trades or loss of opportunity. This is particularly risky during periods of high network congestion or volatility.
