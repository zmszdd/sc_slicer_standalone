Donation attack will break the share's decimal accounting leading to uncapped loss of funds over several epochs

## Finding description

The [`PanopticVaultAccountant::computeNAV`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-06-panoptic/blob/8ef6d867a5fb6ffd1a6cc479a2380a611d452b4a/src/accountants/PanopticVaultAccountant.sol#L94) is used in order to compute the NAV of the portfolio contained in `vault` in terms of its underlying token (which considers the balance of underlying tokens present in the vault as well).

This is used inside the [`HypoVault::fulfillDeposits`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-06-panoptic/blob/8ef6d867a5fb6ffd1a6cc479a2380a611d452b4a/src/HypoVault.sol#L472) and [`HypoVault::fulfillWithdrawals`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-06-panoptic/blob/8ef6d867a5fb6ffd1a6cc479a2380a611d452b4a/src/HypoVault.sol#L516) to calculte the total assets, calculate the shares and update the total supply accordingly:


However, directly reading the contract's underlying balance will lead to serious consequences:

1. First ever `fulfillDeposits` will naturally have `totalAssets` to be calculated as 1 as there are no funds deposited / managed yet.
This can be confirmed from the tests as well where it clearly shows `totalAssets` being 1 as well as if we apply common sense.

2. However, if an attacker frontruns the `fulfillDeposits` call and donates `underlyingToken` here, it would change the `sharesReceived` drastically: 

3. As `sharesReceived` are calculated to be with 6 decimals, it would not increase the `totalSupply` much.
However, the ideal scaling which we would've achieved without donation is now missing and will always stay missing as the denominator is now no longer 1.

Hence, everytime a deposit arrives, the shares received calculation would be scaled similarly:

As total assets keep increasing, a point would arrive where `assetsToFulfill * _totalSupply` < `totalAssets`.
This would lead to `0` sharesReceived.


This would not allow deposits to mint any shares:



Hence, we would see unprecedented loss of funds here.




BONUS FACTOR:
One more thing to consider here is that the amount donated is not fully lost, we can consider the donated amount to being proportionately split between the users:

The `assetsReceived` here would be higher as the `totalAssets` is inflated in nature due to donated funds.

An attacker can simply use the next epoch's `fulfillDeposits` with a very huge deposit, this would allow the attacker to request a withdrawal right before fulfilling withdrawals in the next epoch ( this can be frontran as well ).

Hence, allowing attacker to regain back some of his funds.


## Impact

1. The intended scaling of shares will get broken beyond repair irrespective of the deciamals of the underlying (attack above works for both 18 and 6 decimal ~ USDT / USDC tokens).
2. Loss of funds over several epochs.
