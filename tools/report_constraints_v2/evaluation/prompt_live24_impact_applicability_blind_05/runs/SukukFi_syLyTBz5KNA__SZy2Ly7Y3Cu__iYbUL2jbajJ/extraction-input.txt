Anyone can steal all the assets from users who have a active position with minted shares and have set self allowance for withdraw.

## Finding description and impact

The `redeem()` and `withdraw()` functions of `WERC7575Vault.sol` doesn't check if ``owner == msg.sender`` or does the ``msg.sender`` have allowance for the ``owner shares``.



The only check is for ``spendSelfAllowance(owner, shares);`` but it does nothing.

The ``self allowance`` mechanism is for resolving `` Settlement disputes before withdrawal to prevent carriers from withdrawing during fraud investigation``.

That's why ``self-allowance`` requires ``validator`` signature.

Thus, any ``owner`` who wants to redeem their own ``shares`` for ``assets`` needs to have ``self-allowance``.

Also, this finding by ``V12`` is invalid due to the above reason:
https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-11-sukukfi/blob/main/2025_11_sukukfi_v12.md#incorrect-allowance-spending-in-spendselfallowance

Also, the ``KYC`` can't save this exploit as only ``owner`` needs to be ``KYC'd`` in ``burn()``.


Thus, Any ``attacker`` or Any ``carrier with unresolved dispute`` can steal funds from the vault.

> Due to the nature of the ERC7575 multi-asset vaults, the shares of owner in USDC vault can be used to withdraw from ETH vault.
