## Finding description and impact

The bug here in the contract is the `AtomWallet` treats the trailing 12-byte `validUntil || validAfter` suffix in a 77-byte signature as trusted authorization metadata even though those 12 bytes are not authenticated by the signer. During `_validateSignature`, the contract first extracts `validUntil`, `validAfter`, and the stripped 65-byte raw signature from `userOp.signature`, then recovers the signer using only that 65-byte signature, and finally returns validation data containing the extracted timestamps. Because the recovered signer check does not cover the trailing 12 bytes, any relayer or observer holding a valid signed UserOperation can strip the suffix entirely to make the operation effectively timeless, or rewrite the suffix to make the operation valid earlier or keep it valid longer, without invalidating the signature. As a result, time-limited approvals in `AtomWallet` are not enforceable, and signed wallet actions such as arbitrary calls, token approvals, transfers, or deposit withdrawals can be executed outside the time window the owner intended here where this is came from :
