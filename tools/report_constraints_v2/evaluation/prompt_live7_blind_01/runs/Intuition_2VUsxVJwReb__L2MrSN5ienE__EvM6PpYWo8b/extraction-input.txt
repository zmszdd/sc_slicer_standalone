`AtomWallet` lacks ERC-1271 support, breaking AA functionality for multisig owners

## Finding description and impact

**Root Cause:**
`AtomWallet` operates as an ERC-4337 Smart Contract Wallet (SCW) intended to provide Account Abstraction (AA) features. The authorization mechanism for ERC-4337 `UserOperations` is handled in the `_validateSignature` function. 

Currently, `_validateSignature` rigidly assumes that the `owner` of the `AtomWallet` is an Externally Owned Account (EOA) possessing a private key. It exclusively utilizes `ECDSA.tryRecover()` to derive the signer's address and directly compares it against `owner()`:


Because smart contracts (such as a Gnosis Safe, a DAO Treasury, or even the protocol's default `AtomWarden`) do not possess private keys, they cannot generate standard `r, s, v` ECDSA signatures. Consequently, `ecrecover` will either fail or recover an unrelated EOA address, ensuring that `recovered != owner()` always evaluates to `true`.

**Impact:**
1. **Bricking of Account Abstraction Features**: In the Intuition protocol, high-value `Atoms` representing institutions, DAOs, or community nodes will inherently be controlled via Multi-Signature wallets (e.g., Safe) to ensure security. Once ownership is transferred to a multisig, the `AtomWallet` loses all AA capabilities. The DAO will be permanently unable to route transactions through the `EntryPoint`, meaning they cannot utilize Paymasters for gas sponsorship, batch operations, or automated claim executions.
2. **Default State Incompatibility**: Newly minted `AtomWallets` are by default owned by the `AtomWarden` (which is a smart contract). This implies that in its initial unclaimed state, the wallet inherently cannot interact with the ERC-4337 pipeline.
