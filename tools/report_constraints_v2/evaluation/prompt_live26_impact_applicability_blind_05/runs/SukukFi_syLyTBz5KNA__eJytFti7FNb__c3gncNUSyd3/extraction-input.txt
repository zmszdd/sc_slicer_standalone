Assets Can Be Permanently Stuck in `WERC7575Vault` Prevent Vault Unregistration (DoS)

## Finding description and impact

The `WERC7575Vault` is designed for, 1:1 asset-to-share conversion (with decimal scaling). It relies on the dedicated functions `deposit()` or `mint()` to receive the underlying asset. 

The vulnerability arises because the vault contract does not prevent direct asset transfers to its address. Increases the vault's `totalAssets()` without minting any shares.

When the `WERC7575ShareToken.unregisterVault(asset)` function is called, it performs a crucial safety check:
The stuck assets have no matching shares, meaning no one can call `redeem()` or `withdraw()` to clear them out. The vault has no mechanism to recover these funds, leading to unrecoverable fund and DoS for `unregisterVault()` function
