Zero‑Supply Week Strands Emissions: Unclaimable Rewards in RewardsDistributor

### Summary
Rewards for weeks with zero veHYBR supply become permanently unclaimable. If the distributor is funded and checkpointed before any ve holders exist, that week’s allocation is fixed to a week where all users had zero balance, so no one can ever claim it later. The tokens remain stranded in `RewardsDistributor`.


### Finding Description

Intended:
- Minter funds `RewardsDistributor` and calls `checkpoint_token()`, which apportions tokens across weekly buckets.
- Later, users with veHYBR can claim by summing their weekly pro‑rata share: `balanceOfNFTAt(...) / totalSupplyAtT(...)`.

Actual:
- `_checkpoint_token()` credits the current week’s bucket even if there are no ve holders for that week.
- The claim loop uses historical balance snapshots at that week’s end. If no locks existed for that week, every user’s `balanceOfNFTAt(...)` is zero, so the entire allocation is forever unclaimable.

Why it happens


- If no ve existed at `week_cursor + WEEK - 1`, `balance_of` is 0 for every tokenId, and the week’s `tokens_per_week` allocation is never claimed.

### Impact
- A portion of matured weekly rewards becomes permanently stuck in `RewardsDistributor` whenever funding/checkpointing happens before any ve holders exist for that week (including the first/funding week).
- Long‑term value leakage; misalignment between notified and claimable rewards.

### Likelihood
- Moderate. Deployments and initial funding often occur before any user lock exists. Also possible in any week with zero supply (e.g., migration/maintenance windows).

### Affected Components (concise)
- `ve33/contracts/RewardsDistributor.sol`
  - `_checkpoint_token()`: allocates to weeks regardless of current supply state
  - `_claim/_claimable`: strictly reads end‑of‑week snapshots; zero‑supply weeks produce zero claimable

### Recommendation
- Roll forward zero‑supply allocations: if `totalSupplyAtT(week_end) == 0`, carry `tokens_per_week[week]` into the next week with nonzero supply.
- Alternatively, defer allocation until at least one ve holder exists (gate the first checkpoint).
- Telemetry: assert per epoch that sum(distributed) + undistributed_rollover equals notified.
