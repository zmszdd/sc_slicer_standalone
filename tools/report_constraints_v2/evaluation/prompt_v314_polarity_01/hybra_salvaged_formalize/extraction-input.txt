Incorrect share-minting order in deposit() causes under-minting and loss of funds for new depositors

#### Root cause

In `deposit()`, the contract **locks** the deposited HYBR into the veNFT **before** computing how many gHYBR shares to mint. That makes `totalAssets()` larger by `amount` (or nearly so) when `calculateShares(amount)` is called, which **inflates the denominator** and **mints fewer shares** than fair value for the depositor.

### **Impact**

Whenever `veTokenId != 0`, every new depositor receives **fewer gHYBR shares than fair value**, because their deposit increases `totalAssets()` **before** the share calculation.

This creates a **systematic and deterministic value leak**:

* **Depositors lose claim value:** Each deposit mints fewer shares than the previewed amount (via `calculateShares()`), so new participants receive a smaller proportional stake in the vault.
* **Existing holders are over-rewarded:** The shortfall in minted shares artificially increases the share value of incumbents, effectively redistributing future rewards to them.

> **Note:** The very first depositor (`_totalSupply == 0`) receives the correct 1:1 mint.
> Every subsequent depositor is under-minted proportionally to vault growth.

---

### **Example — Predictable Share Mismatch**

| Metric        | Before Bob’s Deposit | After Bob’s Deposit      |
| ------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `totalAssets` | 100                  | 200 (includes Bob’s 100) |
| `totalSupply` | 100                  | —                        |
| `deposit`     | 100                  | —                        |

**Expected (if `calculateShares` called before deposit):**
`shares = 100 * 100 / 100 = 100`

**Actual (with bug, since `totalAssets` already includes deposit):**
`shares = 100 * 100 / 200 = 50`

**Result:**
Bob receives **50 gHYBR** instead of **100**, losing half of his rightful share.
The missing value silently accrues to existing holders, doubling their per-share entitlement.

This is a **deterministic mismatch** between the preview (`calculateShares`) and actual mint outcome — no race, randomness, or off-chain dependency. Any depositor after the first one will face a consistent under-mint proportional to total vault size.
