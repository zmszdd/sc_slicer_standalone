Early Users Cannot Deposit or Attackers Gain Massive Shares at Low Cost

## Finding Description and Impact

**The HypoVault initialization flaw allows users to exploit the first deposit fulfillment by manipulating the share price calculation through the broken `totalAssets` formula.** In the `fulfillDeposits` function of the HypoVault.sol contract, the vault starts with `totalSupply = 1,000,000` but zero assets, the function calculates `totalAssets = NAV + 1 - assetsDeposited - reservedWithdrawalAssets`, which can either underflow (causing all deposits to fail with reverts) or result in an extremely small positive value like 1～2. When `totalAssets` is tiny, users can deposit minimal amounts (e.g., 499 ETH) and receive massive share allocations (e.g., 499 million shares instead of the normal ~499 shares) through the formula `sharesReceived =  (assetsToFulfill × totalSupply) / totalAssets` = (assetsToFulfill × 1,000,000) / 1`, effectively stealing ownership from future depositors who will receive severely diluted shares at normal market prices.

### Root Cause

The issue stems from the vault's initialization design:

1.**Fixed Initial totalSupply**: The constructor sets `totalSupply = 1_000_000` while `totalAssets` effectively starts at 0
