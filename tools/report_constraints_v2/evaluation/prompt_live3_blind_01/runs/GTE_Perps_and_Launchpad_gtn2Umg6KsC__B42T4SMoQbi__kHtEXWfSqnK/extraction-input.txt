Incorrect Reward Distribution on Token Transfers Post-Graduation Causes Reward Loss

## Summary
The launchpad rewards system distributes earned fees to users when they unbond or claim. However, after token graduation, when users transfer tokens (triggering unstaking), the rewards are incorrectly sent to the launchpad contract instead of the user.

In `LaunchToken._decreaseFeeShares()`, token transfers post-graduation call `launchpad.decreaseStake(account, amount)`, which invokes `Distributor.decreaseStake()`. This function unstakes the user and calls `_distributeAssets()` to transfer rewards, but `_distributeAssets()` sends tokens to `msg.sender`, which is the launchpad (as `decreaseStake()` is restricted to `onlyLaunchpad`).

Consequently, users lose access to their rewards, which accumulate in the launchpad contract.

## Root cause
The root cause is that `_distributeAssets()` assumes `msg.sender` is the reward recipient, but when called from `decreaseStake()`, `msg.sender` is the launchpad, not the user.


## Impact
Users cannot receive rewards earned during bonding when transferring tokens after graduation, leading to loss of funds.

> [!NOTE]
> `claimRewards()` correctly sends to `msg.sender`, but automatic unstaking on transfer does not.
