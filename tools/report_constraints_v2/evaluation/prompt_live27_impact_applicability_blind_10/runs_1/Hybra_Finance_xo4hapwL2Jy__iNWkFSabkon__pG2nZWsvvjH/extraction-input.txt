`votingEscrow` NFTs lose rewards upon splitting-merging

## Finding description and impact
in `VotingEscrow`, any token with pending rewards merged as `from` or splitted will lose all of its earned rewards

this happens due to the fact that when we merge a `from` Id or we split a main NFT Id we  `_burn` that NFT, and hence its owner will be `address(0)` and hence claims will be lost since amounts will be transferred to `address(0)`
this is not an out-of-scope bug since it doesn't originate from the `RewardsDistributor` but it comes from not calling claim upon any merge/splitting.

Also this wouldn't be a user mistake since he assumes that those cases will be handled as developer said in his ` ve(3,3) Epoch Cycle, Rotation, and Reward Distribution / Rebase` are of concerns

but in addition to users losing their rewards, `gHybr` contract is also affected.

since `gHybr` contract settles withdrawals through splitting, this would happen.
1. Users deposits 100e18 to `governanceHybr`
2. `governanceHybr` earns `Hybr` rewards
3. one user is willing withdraw, `claimRewards()` hasn't been called by the operator yet
4. the user withdraw and the old ve Id owned by `governanceHybr` gets splitted and loses all old `hybr` rewards

The above scenario is more reaslistic since it doesn't involve a naive user mistake of not claiming before splitting
### Impact
Loss of user rewards upon merging/splitting

Loss of rebase amounts given to `gHybr`
