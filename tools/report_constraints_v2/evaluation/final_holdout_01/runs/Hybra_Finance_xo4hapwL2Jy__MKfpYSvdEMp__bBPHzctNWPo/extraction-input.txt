Rewards Can Be Stolen by Anyone by Calling claimFees

## Finding description and impact
The `_claimFees` function, which can be called by anyone via the public `claimFees` function, is designed to collect trading fees from the CL pool and send them to a bribe contract. However, it calculates the amount of collected fees by checking the contract's total balance of the pool's tokens (`token0` and `token1`) after calling `clPool.collectFees()`. 

This is a critical flaw because rewards are also held in this contract. If the `rewardToken` is the same as `token0` or `token1` (a very common use case, e.g., rewarding a HYBR/ETH pool with HYBR), the function will treat all reward tokens as fees. As a result, anyone can call `claimFees()` and drain the gauge's reward token balance, sending it to the `internal_bribe` contract. This effectively steals the rewards from the liquidity providers who earned them. Subsequent attempts by users to claim their rewards will fail because the funds are no longer in the gauge.
