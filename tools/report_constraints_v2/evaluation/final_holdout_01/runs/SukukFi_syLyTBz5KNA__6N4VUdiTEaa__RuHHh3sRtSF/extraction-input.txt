Paused mode ineffective: batchTransfers, rBatchTransfers, and rBalance adjustment functions lack `whenNotPaused`, allowing privileged balance/rBalance changes while paused

## Finding description and impact

The batch settlement and revenue adjustment entry points (`batchTransfers`, `rBatchTransfers`, `adjustrBalance`, and `cancelrBalanceAdjustment`) all describe “Contract must not be paused” in their requirement comments, but none of them include the `whenNotPaused` modifier. This leaves the Pausable circuit breaker unused for these privileged flows even though less-privileged mint/burn and transfer functions are correctly gated by `whenNotPaused`.

Impact 

After the owner calls `pause()`, the validator can still net balances through `batchTransfers`/`rBatchTransfers`, moving user funds without restriction, and the revenue admin can continue rewriting restricted balances via `adjustrBalance`/`cancelrBalanceAdjustment`. The intended emergency stop therefore fails to halt the highest privilege operations, allowing continued asset shuffling or accounting changes during an incident response window
