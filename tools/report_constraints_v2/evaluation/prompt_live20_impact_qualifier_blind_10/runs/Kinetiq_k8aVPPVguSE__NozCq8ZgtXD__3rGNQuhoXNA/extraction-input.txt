`ValidatorManager#reportSlashingEvent()` doesn't slash pending withdrawals

## Finding description and impact
- A staker can call [`StakingManager#queueWithdrawal()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-04-kinetiq/blob/main/src/StakingManager.sol#L256-L296) to create a HYPE withdrawal request(A withdrawal operation will be created).
- The withdrawal operation will be executed by the operator of the protocol via calling [`StakingManager#processL1Operations()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-04-kinetiq/blob/main/src/StakingManager.sol#L627C14-L659)
- The withdrawal request can only be confirmed after 7 days.
- If a slash event happens to the delegated validator in 7 days, any pending withdrawal should be entitled to be slashed.

However, the Kinetiq protocol lacks a mechanism to handle slash events on pending withdrawals, queued withdrawals prior to the [`ValidatorManager#reportSlashingEvent()`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-04-kinetiq/blob/main/src/ValidatorManager.sol#L427-L438) call could avoid being slashed even they are pending. Meanwhile, all remaining stakers, including those who staked after slashing event, could face repeated slashing after queued withdrawals are confirmed:
As can be seen, the amount of HYPE was calculated at the time of being queued, and it doesn't account any slashing event happens between queuing time and confirming time.
