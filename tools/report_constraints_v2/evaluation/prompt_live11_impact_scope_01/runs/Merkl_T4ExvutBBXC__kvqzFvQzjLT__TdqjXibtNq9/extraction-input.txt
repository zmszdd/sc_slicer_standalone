Net reward floor can be bypassed because minimum is checked before fees, causing underfunded campaigns.

## Finding description and impact

**Description:**  
The guardian-configured whitelist floor `rewardTokenMinAmounts` is enforced on the **gross** [amount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-11-merkl/blob/e8c1d6f5a91c1144571c3ed238358a2b032dd32e/contracts/DistributionCreator.sol#L527-L535) supplied by the campaign creator, but protocol fees are computed and subtracted [after](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-11-merkl/blob/e8c1d6f5a91c1144571c3ed238358a2b032dd32e/contracts/DistributionCreator.sol#L620-L628) this check. The resulting **net** amount (`campaignAmountMinusFees`) is what gets stored in [newCampaign.amount](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-11-merkl/blob/e8c1d6f5a91c1144571c3ed238358a2b032dd32e/contracts/DistributionCreator.sol#L537-L539) and actually distributed, yet there is no second validation that this net amount still satisfies the minimum per-epoch constraint. As a result, a campaign that is exactly at the minimum per hour when measured on the gross amount will pass validation, but once fees are applied the stored/distributed amount falls below `rewardTokenMinAmounts`, breaking the intended invariant.

**Impact:**  
Campaigns can be created that appear to satisfy the guardian’s minimum emission requirement but in practice emit less than `rewardTokenMinAmounts` per epoch:

- Recipients are systematically underpaid relative to the whitelist floor and to what governance/guardian intended to enforce.    
- The protocol’s policy lever (“no campaign below X per epoch”) is weakened: non-zero fees silently push actual emissions below the configured threshold whenever creators choose the minimum “valid” gross amount.

There is no direct theft of funds beyond the configured fee, but this constitutes a broken protocol-level guarantee and can lead to underfunded reward programs and incorrect economic assumptions.
