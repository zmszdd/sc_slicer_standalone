`function tokenize` is vulnurable to frontrun.

## Finding description and impact

The tokenize function is vulnerable to frontrunning attacks. It allows any user to call the function with a _url parameter and receive ownership or control of a metaCoinAddress associated with that URL. However, the function does not enforce any mechanism to authenticate or prove that the caller is the legitimate owner or originator of the _url. As a result, an attacker can observe a pending transaction attempting to tokenize a specific URL and quickly submit their transaction with a higher gas price to frontrun the original user.

An attacker can front-run legitimate users and tokenize URLs that do not belong to them. This results in:

* Loss of ownership for the intended user over the tokenized asset.
* Reputation risk for the platform, as users can lose trust due to the lack of fairness and protection against frontrunning.
