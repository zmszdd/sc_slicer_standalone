# Reward Emissions Permanently Stuck in Gauge When Total Supply is Zero During Accrual Periods

## Finding Description  
The GaugeV2 contract's reward accrual mechanism fails to properly account for emissions when the total supply of staked tokens is zero, resulting in reward tokens being transferred to the contract but never becoming claimable by users. The issue arises in the reward distribution logic where rewards are accrued proportionally to the total supply. Specifically, in the [`rewardPerToken`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-10-hybra-finance/blob/74c2b93e8a4796c1d41e1fbaa07e40b426944c44/ve33/contracts/GaugeV2.sol#L180-L186) function, if _totalSupply == 0, it returns the stored value without adding the time-based accrual (lastTimeRewardApplicable() - lastUpdateTime) * rewardRate * 1e18 / _totalSupply. Meanwhile, the updateReward modifier, called during user interactions or notifications, advances lastUpdateTime to the current applicable time regardless of supply. This skips reward accumulation for empty periods, and subsequent notifyRewardAmount calls only consider leftovers from the remaining time after the advanced lastUpdateTime, leaving previously skipped rewards unaccounted for and trapped in the contract's balance. The highest impact scenario occurs when the gauge remains empty for an entire reward duration (one week), causing the full epoch's emissions to become permanently unclaimable.

In the[ `rewardPerToken`](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-10-hybra-finance/blob/74c2b93e8a4796c1d41e1fbaa07e40b426944c44/ve33/contracts/GaugeV2.sol#L180-L186) function of GaugeV2, when _totalSupply == 0, the function skips the addition of new accrued rewards and returns only the stored value, while `lastUpdateTime` is still updated forward in the updateReward modifier, preventing retroactive accrual of skipped periods.

1. Users call withdraw or withdrawAll to remove all staked tokens from the gauge, setting _totalSupply to zero.  
2. Time passes without any deposits, and during this period, notifyRewardAmount
 may be called by the distribution contract to add new rewards, but accrual is skipped in rewardPerToken
 due to zero supply while lastUpdateTime advances.  
3. Even after new deposits occur and users interact (e.g., via `deposit`), the skipped rewards remain unaccrued and unclaimable, as future calculations do not retroactively include the empty period's emissions.

## Impact:  
Entire epochs of reward emissions can become permanently stuck in the contract, leading to protocol fund loss without recovery, with higher impact in low-liquidity gauges that frequently empty, potentially wasting significant token allocations over time.
