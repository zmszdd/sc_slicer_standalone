The current logic of the contract is that when LaunchToken reaches BONDING_SUPPLY on a buy, the contract should automatically deposit 20% of the token into the UniSwapV2 pool and it should also deposit all of the quoteReserve it holds. 

However, an attacker can permanently prevent a token from graduating with 1 wei direct deposit into the uniswap pair. In uniswapV2 a pair can be created easily by providing the LaunchToken address and quote address.

How will the attack work:

1. Lets say that a token is near its graduation, the attacker can deploy the pair (token,quote) before the graduation.

2. He can then make a direct deposit of 1 wei into the pair and call sync, this will bump the internal reserves of tokenA (depends on which is the smaller token, in my ex. I will make the quote token token A) to 1 wei.

3. When graduation comes in _graduate we make a call to _createPairAndSwapRemaining, inside that function we try to add liquidity to the pair by calling ` uniV2Router.addLiquidity`, however given the attacker initiated the attack before that, the call will fail, making the whole transaction revert, disabling graduation forever.

## Impact
Permanent DoS in a way that the contract is stopped performing its main functionality, given that in the launch of the new contract a lot of tokens will be created as a lot of users will want to arbitrage from the new functionality GTE offers, an attacker can make a massive attack by suppling 1 wei of quote to each token made. Given that this bug will only appear when one of the newly deployed tokens reach their BONDING_SUPPLY, this can be catastrophic, as users will be forced to pull out all of their quote. 

I think this is a High as it breaks core contract functionality. The attack is cheap and easy.
