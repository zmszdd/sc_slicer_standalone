Liquidity Provider Value Leakage in Launchpad Fork due to Reserve Shaving Fee Mechanism

## Finding description and impact

### Description

The GTELaunchpadV2Pair contract is a fork of the Uniswap V2 pair contract and introduces a modified fee mechanism inside the _update function. Instead of charging swap fees from the input amount (standard in Uniswap V2), the fork reduces the reserve update amount directly.

This subtle change breaks the standard constant product invariant assumptions (x * y = k). As shown in the test_SingleSwap_LPValueLeak, when an identical swap is performed on both the forked pair and a reference Uniswap like pair, the liquidity provider’s (LP’s) portfolio value declines more in the fork than in the reference.

In effect, the reserve shaving fee mechanism leaks value from LPs to arbitrageurs and traders because the post swap reserves do not balance properly against the invariant. Over time, this results in:
•	Systematic erosion of LP portfolio value beyond the intended 0.3% fee.
•	Arbitrage opportunities that allow external traders to repeatedly extract value at the expense of LPs.

When a trader moves the price away from equilibrium and an arbitrageur backruns the trade, LPs in the fork lose even more value than in the reference. The reserve shaving mechanism amplifies arbitrage profit opportunities at the direct expense of LPs, effectively making LPs subsidize arbitrageurs.

## Impact
•	LPs consistently lose more value per trade compared to standard Uniswap V2.
•	Arbitrageurs capture the leaked value in a risk free manner through rebalancing trades.
•	LPs may be disincentivized from providing liquidity, degrading market depth and stability.
