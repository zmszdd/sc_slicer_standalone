`TokenWrapper.transferFrom` emits a non-standard `Transfer` event (`msg.sender` instead of `from`)

## Finding description and impact
`ERC-20` mandates that the `Transfer` event always identify the token holder whose balance decreases in the `from` field


But in `TokenWrapper.sol` the `transferFrom` implementation emits the spender (`msg.sender`) as `from`, rather than the approved token owner (`from` parameter).  



## Impact
ERC-20 non-compliance – automated tooling may treat the token as invalid or malfunction when reconstructing balances from events.  
Incorrect attribution – wallets, indexers, subgraphs, analytics and airdrop scripts will record the spender as the debited account, corrupting historical data, balances, and accounting.  
Operational risk – once deployed, the incorrect events are immutable; integrators must add special-case logic or accept corrupted data.
