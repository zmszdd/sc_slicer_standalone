Title: Minimum Reward Validation Uses Gross Amount Instead of Net Amount After Fees

## Finding Description

In the `_createCampaign` function, the contract performs minimum reward validation using the gross `newCampaign.amount` before deducting protocol fees:

However, the actual campaign funding applied to participants is the net amount after fees:

This mismatch allows campaigns that pass the gross amount check to have a net reward per hour below the intended minimum once fees are applied, violating the expected economic guarantees.

## Impact

- Campaigns may distribute rewards below the minimum intended threshold.

- Economic incentives for participants can be weakened, reducing campaign effectiveness and trust in the protocol.

- Protocol rules for minimum funding are bypassable, potentially leading to misaligned campaign rewards.

## Root cause

The contract validates minimum rewards against the gross amount before fees are deducted but uses the net amount after fees for actual distribution.
