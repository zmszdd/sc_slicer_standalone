Premium Calculation Logic Error - Inconsistency of Premium Token1 Calculation Formula

## Finding Description and Impact
There is a critical inconsistency in the premium exposure calculation formula between token0 and token1 in the `computeNAV()` function in the `PanopticVaultAccountant` contract.
Where it should be:
This will cause vaults with premium positions to experience significant NAV miscalculations. Any vault that has a net positive premium on token1 will lose value in the NAV calculation. Incorrect NAVs can lead to premature or late liquidations.

Impact Scenario
- Premium Token0: Short 100 ETH, Long 30 ETH → Exposure = +70 ETH ✅
- Premium Token1: Short 50,000 USDC, Long 20,000 USDC → Exposure = -30,000 USDC ❌ (should be +30,000 USDC)
- Total Exposure: 70 ETH + (-30,000 USDC) = negative → NAV = 0 (after Math.max)
- Loss: 30,070 ether in NAV calculation
