Epoch-End veTRUST Snapshot Includes Next-Epoch Boundary Locks, Allowing Boundary Lockers to Claim Prior-Epoch Rewards

## Finding description and impact

### Root cause

`totalBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd` and `userBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd` pass `_epochTimestampEnd(epoch)` directly to VotingEscrow's `_totalSupply` and `_balanceOf`:


This is incorrect because `_epochTimestampEnd(n)` is the first timestamp of epoch `n+1`, not the last timestamp of epoch `n`:


Under `epochAtTimestamp()`, this timestamp is classified as epoch `n+1`, not epoch `n`. But because VotingEscrow's binary search uses inclusive `<=` comparison (VotingEscrow.sol:616), a checkpoint created at exactly this timestamp satisfies `ts <= _epochTimestampEnd(n)` and IS included in epoch `n`'s snapshot. This means any lock created at `epochTimestampStart(n+1)` inflates the reward snapshot for epoch `n`, and the attacker can then call `claimRewards()` to extract real native tokens via `SatelliteEmissionsController.transfer()`.

### Attack scenario

**Step 1:** Epoch `n` is running. Alice has TRUST locked throughout the entire epoch.

**Step 2:** At `block.timestamp == epochTimestampEnd(n)`, the attacker calls `create_lock()` with a large TRUST amount. On Base L2 (2-second block times), submitting the transaction in the right block is trivial. The VotingEscrow checkpoint is created with `ts = epochTimestampEnd(n)`.

**Step 3:** The attacker calls `claimRewards()`. The contract queries `prevEpoch = n` and computes:
- `userBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd(attacker, n)` → non-zero (includes the boundary lock)
- `totalBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd(n)` → inflated by the attacker's lock
- The attacker receives a pro-rata share of epoch `n`'s emissions as real native tokens via `SatelliteEmissionsController.transfer()`

**Step 4:** The attacker's locked TRUST tokens remain in the VotingEscrow and are withdrawable after the lock expires. The attacker earned unearned yield on capital whose position was not active during the target epoch.

### Impact

1. **Unearned reward extraction**: The attacker calls `claimRewards()` and receives real native token transfers from the SatelliteEmissionsController for an epoch during which their position was not active. In the PoC, the attacker extracts ~504,854 TRUST (~50.5% of epoch emissions) by locking an equal amount at the boundary.

2. **Reward dilution**: Legitimate bonders who locked TRUST throughout the epoch have their rewards diluted proportionally because the prior-epoch denominator includes next-epoch boundary locks. Alice loses ~50.5% of her expected rewards in the PoC.

3. **Repeatable across epochs**: The attack opportunity recurs at each epoch boundary and can be repeated subject to available capital and lock-duration constraints.

## Summary

`totalBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd` and `userBondedBalanceAtEpochEnd` snapshot veTRUST at `epochTimestampEnd(n)`, which equals `epochTimestampStart(n+1)`. Because VotingEscrow's binary search uses inclusive `<=` matching, a lock created at this boundary timestamp is counted toward epoch `n`'s reward distribution. An attacker can lock TRUST at the exact epoch boundary and call `claimRewards()` to extract real native tokens for an epoch during which their position was not active, diluting legitimate bonders' rewards. The fix is to snapshot at `epochTimestampEnd(n) - 1`.
