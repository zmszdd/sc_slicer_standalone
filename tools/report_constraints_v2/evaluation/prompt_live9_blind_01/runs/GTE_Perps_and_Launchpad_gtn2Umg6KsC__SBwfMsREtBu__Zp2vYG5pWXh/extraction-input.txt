Bonding curve does not round in favour of the protocol

## Finding description and impact

When users buy from the bonding curve, the amount they pay is rounded down on SimpleBondingCurve.sol:202-210:


When they sell, the amount they get in return also rounds down. The problem is that repeated calls to `Launchpad::buy` (which all suffer from the rounding down issue), may be followed by a single call to `Launchpad::sell` which suffers a lesser effect from rounding. This results in a potential profit for an attacker. Also, it has the potential be cause the protocol to be frozen (for a specific token) in a situation where there are few `quoteReserves` in the curve, which could occur in the early stages of the token launch or if there are a lot of users selling back their tokens to the protocol. This is because on every call to `Launchpad::buy` or `Launchpad::sell` an event is emitted on Launchpad.sol:600:


The call to `curve.quoteBoughtByCurve(token)` could be problematic:


We see that if `reserves[token].quoteReserve < VIRTUAL_QUOTE`, the function reverts. This could actually occur because users can take more quote tokens out of the protocol than they put in, due to the rounding issue. Potentially, all holders attempt to sell their tokens but the last seller will be unable to sell due to the revert shown above.
