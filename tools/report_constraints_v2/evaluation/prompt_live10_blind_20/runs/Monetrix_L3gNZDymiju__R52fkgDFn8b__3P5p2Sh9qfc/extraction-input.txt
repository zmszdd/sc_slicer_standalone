PM hedge registration can brick totalBacking() and settle() by adding a vaultSupplied entry before the HyperCore 0x811 supplied slot exists

When PM is enabled, `executeHedge()` calls `notifyVaultSupply(spotToken, params.perpAsset)`. This immediately adds a `vaultSupplied` entry to `MonetrixAccountant`.

After that, every `totalBackingSigned()` call iterates `vaultSupplied` and performs a strict `PrecompileReader.suppliedBalance()` read against HyperCore precompile 0x811. If the hedge order was submitted but the L1 fill or PM auto-supply never happens, the corresponding 0x811 slot may not exist. The strict read then reverts with:

```PrecompileReader: supplied balance read failed```
That revert bricks:

- `accountant.totalBacking()`
- `accountant.distributableSurplus()`
- `accountant.settleDailyPnL()`
- `vault.settle()`

The only recovery path shown in the PoC is privileged manual cleanup through `removeSuppliedEntry(false, index)`.

Broken invariant: a submitted hedge order must not be enough to make protocol accounting and settlement unavailable. Accounting should reflect confirmed retrievable backing, not assume an external PM supplied slot exists.
