Expired Orders Cause POST_ONLY Order Starvation

## Summary
The `maxNumLimitsPerSide` constraint enforces a cap on the number of orders per side in the limit order book. However, expired or orders are not explicitly filtered or removed before enforcing this constraint. This oversight allows for griefing the system by occupying book space with expired orders, effectively preventing valid limit orders from being posted.

## Finding Description
The `_processLimitAskOrder()` function enforces a hard cap on the number of active ask orders:

However, `ds.askTree.size()` includes all orders, regardless of whether they are expired or actionable. There is no logic to remove, or invalidate expired limit orders before this capacity check.

As a result, more competitive and valid limit orders may be denied simply because the book is perceived as full, even though many of the resident orders are inactive or expired.

  1. freezing one side of the book.
