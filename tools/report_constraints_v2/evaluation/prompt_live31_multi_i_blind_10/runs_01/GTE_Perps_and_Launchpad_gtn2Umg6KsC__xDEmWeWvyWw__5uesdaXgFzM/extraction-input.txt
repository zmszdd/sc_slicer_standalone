Truncation in reward distribution `getAccRewardsPerShare`  causes permanent fund loss

## Finding description 

The rewards distribution mechanism suffers a critical precision loss issue, 
when distributing reward amounts to a large number of shareholders,  
causing complete loss of funds due to truncation in the `getAccRewardsPerShare` calculation.

Root Cause

The `getAccRewardsPerShare` function [here](https://github.com/code-423n4/2025-08-gte-perps/blob/main/contracts/launchpad/libraries/RewardsTracker.sol#L209-L228) in `RewardsTracker.sol` uses integer division that truncates to zero when `(pendingRewards * PRECISION_FACTOR) < totalShares` , 
permanently losing reward amounts.


Note : total shares can be upto 800 Million (8e26) in a fully sold out bonding curve. 


Provided the distributor contract has a non-zero `pendingBaseRewards` or `pendingQuoteRewards` ,
The `getAccRewardsPerShare` function is triggered thru `update` every time an user claims or transfers their launchTokens purchased from bonding curves.

Meanwhile, the `GTELaunchpadV2Pair` contract continuously adds small reward amounts to the distributor in the form of swap fees. 

Under this scenario,  
all these rewards are permanently lost due to the truncation issue as described in below examples.

Example A: USDC (6 decimals) Quote Token

Example B: WETH (18 decimals) Quote Token

## Impact and Likelihood

1. Permanent loss of funds - Truncated rewards are not recoverable.
2. All swap fees distributed thru the pair contract are lost, 
   creating significant cumulative losses over time
3. Fee share holders lose earned rewards from AMM trading activity.
4. Unusable reward mechanism 
5. Affects every launchToken graduated thru bonding curve

## Recommended mitigation steps

Several ways to fix 

Method 1 : Use a higher precision factor

Method 2: Reward accumulation threshold
