previewwithdraw reverts on max-asset withdrawals due to share truncation

## Finding description and impact
convertToShares truncates the shares returned to depositors. This means after any realistic deposit, totalAssets is strictly greater than `HALF_SLOPE * totalShares²` by 1-2 wei. When anyone calls `previewWithdraw(totalAssets, totalAssets, totalShares)`, the internal PRBMath subtraction underflows and reverts
When depositing assets, _convertToShares computes the corresponding shares by taking a floor square root
Because the sqrt operation floors the result, the vault retains slightly more assets than the  curve requires
Since minDeposit = 1e16, virtually every real deposit produces non-round shares and creates this gap instantly.(Note this is submitted assuming it will be used like a helper)
## Impact
- Triggers after the first realistic deposit
- Any function using(inherting) totalAssets to previewWithdraw always reverts
- Funds are not lost —redeem(totalShares) continues to work  but the entire withdraw path is broken from first use
