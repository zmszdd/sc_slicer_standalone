FOK fill orders revert when not a multiple of lotSize

### Summary
Under CLOB Fill Rounding of the documentation: 
> "On CLOB fill, filled amounts are rounded down to the nearest lot. FOK fill orders _should_ not revert if only the amount rounded off is left unfilled"

However, it is the case that FOK fill orders **will revert** even if only the amount rounded off is left unfilled.

### Details
The `currMatch.matchedAmount` is a multiple of lot size as it is derived from

If the incoming order has amount that is not a multiple of lot size, the line `incomingOrder.amount -= currMatch.matchedAmount;` will return a non-zero amount and therefore revert with `FOKOrderNotFilled()`.

`if (args.fillOrderType == FillOrderType.FILL_OR_KILL && newOrder.amount > 0) revert FOKOrderNotFilled();`

Therefore, the FOK order is reverted even though only the amount rounded off is left unfilled.

### Remediation
Round the incoming order down to the nearest lot size
