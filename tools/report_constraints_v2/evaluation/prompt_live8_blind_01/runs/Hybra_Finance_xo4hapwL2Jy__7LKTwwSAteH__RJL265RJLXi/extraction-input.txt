GaugeManager warehouses emissions with zero total weight

## Finding description and location
Minter pulls newly minted HYBR into `GaugeManager` and only updates `index` when `totalWeight > 0`. If no gauges have votes yet, the emission sits on the contract balance, `index` never increments, and that tranche is lost forever even after voting begins.

## Impact
When no gauges have received votes yet (`totalWeight == 0`), the newly minted HYBR sits in `GaugeManager` without ever increasing `index`. Later epochs distribute normally, but the initial emissions remain orphaned. This permanently shrinks the reward pool and breaks accounting assumptions that minted tokens will flow into gauges.
