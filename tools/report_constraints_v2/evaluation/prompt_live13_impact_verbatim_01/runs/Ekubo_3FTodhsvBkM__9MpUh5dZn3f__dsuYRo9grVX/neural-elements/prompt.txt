CANONICAL P/I/V CONTRACT
P — Preconditions
Definition: Report-supported scenario facts that hold before the checked action or state transition and delimit when the reported violation is claimed.
Includes: actor or authorization state; relevant prior protocol state; input, asset, value, time, order, or bound conditions.
Excludes: the checked action itself; implementation root cause; desired safety behavior; vulnerable return, revert, post-state, mismatch, loss, or downstream effect.
Formal role: A conjunction of report-grounded predicates evaluated before V.

I — Report-claimed safety property
Definition: The narrowest report-supported property that should hold in the stated scope when its applicability condition is met.
Includes: authorization, bound, value, state-correspondence, conservation, monotonicity, ordering, temporal, integrity, or progress relations; an explicit applicability condition and desired observable relation when needed.
Excludes: a system-wide slogan not supported by the report; the implementation root cause; the vulnerable behavior presented as desired behavior.
Formal role: Usually applicability_condition implies desired_relation.

V — Direct violation witness
Definition: A report-supported, directly observable behavior, return, or state/value change claimed for the scenario, defined independently of I. Whether it violates I must be checked by the P AND V AND I check; that contradiction is not assumed by the definition of V.
Includes: unauthorized completion or state change; forbidden success or failure; out-of-bound value or incorrect before/after relation; a direct mismatch between expected and observed behavior.
Excludes: implementation root cause by itself; generic severity; downstream profit, loss, or denial when it is only a consequence.
Formal role: A constructive report-grounded witness checked against P and I.
